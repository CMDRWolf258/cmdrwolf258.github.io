04

Signal in the Names

Many sites in 10-16 use names inspired by Tool songs, lyrics, and references. Over time that pattern has become more than a naming joke; it is part of the colony's visual and cultural identity.

Saturn Ascending, Eon Blue Apocalypse, Rival Monkeys Foundry, Rabbits with Glasses Agricultural, Tertiary Lens Observatory, and Photon Relay Facility all belong to that continuing naming language.

05

Development Log

The colony network has grown incrementally through new starports, surface sites, settlements, and specialized support facilities. Each completed project changes the practical shape of the system and adds another layer to its history.

This node will continue to expand with ownership data, economic roles, screenshots, construction milestones, and new facilities as they come online.
