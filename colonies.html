04

Naming Identity

Many locations in 10-16 use custom names inspired by Tool songs, lyrics, and references. This naming pattern has become part of the identity of the system and its colonies.

Examples include Saturn Ascending, Eon Blue Apocalypse, Rival Monkeys Foundry, Rabbits with Glasses Agricultural, Tertiary Lens Observatory, and Photon Relay Facility.
