01

Origin & Identity

10-16 was first discovered by CMDR Wolf258 and later selected as the anchor point for a long-term colonization project. What began as a personal discovery has developed into a working home system with its own infrastructure, economy, operational routines, and history.

Its identity is equal parts exploration archive and active frontier: a place to build, mine, manage factions, test colony development, and preserve the story of the system as it changes.

02

World Profile

The system contains an Earth-like world, multiple landable bodies, ringed worlds, and extensive surface resource signals. These features make 10-16 useful not only as a home system, but as a compact operating environment for exploration, mining, trade, and colony development.

Multiple Platinum hotspots and a broad catalogue of surveyed surface deposits give mining a permanent place in the system's economic role.

03

Colonial Development

Construction has transformed 10-16 into a large colony network with orbital starports, industrial hubs, settlements, tourism infrastructure, and specialized support facilities.

Population, economic capability, technology, wealth, and system development continue to evolve as new sites come online and daily player activity shapes the colony.

05

Resource Network

Surface mining sites are catalogued by commodity, body, signal number, coordinates, and known mining-rig density. Strong locations are promoted to preferred status so the archive can function as a practical field reference instead of a simple discovery log.

Ring mining, surface extraction, local market data, and infrastructure all feed into the broader goal of making 10-16 a self-contained and useful operating system.

06

Archive Mission

This archive exists to preserve the continuing history of 10-16: discovery, construction, population growth, infrastructure, faction activity, mining surveys, economic experiments, and visual milestones.

The system is still changing. The archive is designed to change with it.
