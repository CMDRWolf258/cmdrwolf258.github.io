System Overview

NGC 2546 Sector UZ-G d10-16, commonly referred to as 10-16, is the primary home system of CMDR Wolf258 and the focus of an ongoing colonization and development project.

01

Discovery & Identity

10-16 was first discovered by CMDR Wolf258 and later became the center of a long-term colonization project. The system is treated as a personal home system and archive of its ongoing development.

The system includes an Earth-like world along with several landable and ringed bodies, giving it both practical value and a distinctive visual identity.

02

Colonization

Development has included orbital starports, planetary ports, settlements, industrial facilities, tourism infrastructure, and supporting installations throughout the system.

The system is connected to nearby colonized systems through a chain of settlements and systems developed within the colonization jump-distance limits.

04

Resources & Mining

The system contains significant mining opportunities, including Platinum-bearing rings and a large number of surveyed surface mining locations.

Surface sites are being catalogued by commodity, planetary body, signal number, coordinates, and mining-rig density. These records will eventually be accessible through the dedicated Mining section of this archive.

05

Development History

10-16 continues to evolve as new infrastructure is constructed, system population grows, faction influence changes, and additional mining and exploration data is collected.

This archive is intended to preserve that development over time, including stations, screenshots, economic changes, BGS history, construction projects, and notable discoveries.
