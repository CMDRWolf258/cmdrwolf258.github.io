System Overview

NGC 2546 Sector UZ-G d10-16, commonly known as 10-16, is the primary home system of CMDR Wolf258 and the center of an ongoing colonization, infrastructure, mining, and Background Simulation project.

01

Discovery & Identity

10-16 was first discovered by CMDR Wolf258 and later selected as the center of a long-term colonization project. The system has since become the commander's primary home system and the focus of ongoing development.

Its identity is shaped by a mix of exploration, colonization, mining, station construction, faction management, and custom naming throughout the system.

02

Notable System Features

10-16 contains an Earth-like world, multiple landable bodies, ringed worlds, extensive surface-mining opportunities, and a growing network of orbital and surface infrastructure.

The system also contains multiple Platinum hotspots and a large number of surveyed surface resource signals, making mining an important part of its long-term economic role.

03

Colonization & Development

Development has expanded 10-16 from a discovered system into a large colony network containing major starports, industrial hubs, settlements, installations, tourism infrastructure, and specialized support facilities.

Population and system development have grown alongside construction, while economic and security conditions are actively managed through continued player activity.

Mining & Resources

Mining is one of the defining resource activities in 10-16. The system contains valuable ring-mining opportunities as well as an extensive catalogue of surveyed surface deposits.

Surface sites are being recorded by commodity, planetary body, signal number, coordinates, and known mining-rig density. Preferred locations are maintained separately in the Mining section of the archive.
