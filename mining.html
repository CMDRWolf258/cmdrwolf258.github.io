Survey Notes

Surface mining locations are catalogued by body and signal number. When a particularly strong site is found, exact coordinates and the number of workable mining rigs are also recorded.

The long-term goal is to turn this archive into a searchable commodity reference that can quickly identify the best available mining location for a selected resource.
