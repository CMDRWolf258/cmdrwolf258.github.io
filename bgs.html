Operational Doctrine

BGS work in 10-16 is deliberate rather than reactive. Influence is shaped to keep useful factions viable, preserve room for future moves, and position selected factions for strategic station ownership without destabilizing the system unnecessarily.

Missions, bounty vouchers, trade, mining, and other actions are treated as controlled inputs. The goal is not simply to push one bar upward — it is to maintain a system that can still be maneuvered tomorrow.

03

Platinum Market Objective

A major economic objective is to maintain the strongest sustainable Platinum buy market possible inside 10-16, taking advantage of the system's local Platinum resources and industrial infrastructure.

Boom, Civil Liberty, ownership, faction behavior, and other state conditions are monitored as part of that strategy rather than treated as isolated BGS events.
